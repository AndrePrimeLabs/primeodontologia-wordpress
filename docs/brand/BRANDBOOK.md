## 1. Brand Overview

Prime Odontologia is a specialized dental clinic based in Belo Horizonte that focuses on digital dentistry and preventive oral health. The clinic primarily provides Invisalign treatments for children, teens, and adults, using advanced 3D scanning and virtual planning to deliver predictable aesthetic and functional results.

### Official Slogan & Tagline
> **"Conquiste o sorriso que você sempre sonhou com a tecnologia do Invisalign."**

## 3. Typography

- **Primary Typeface:** **Raleway**
  - Used for headlines, hero titles, subheaders, and UI labels.
  - Weights: SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800).
  - Letter spacing: `-0.01em` on headlines, `+0.08em` on uppercase labels.
- **Secondary Typeface:** **Open Sans**
  - Used for body text, clinical descriptions, FAQs, and long-form editorial copy.
  - Weights: Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700).
  - Line height: `1.6`.

## 5. Imagery & Visual Language

- High-definition before/after case studies displaying healthy, natural smile transformations.
- Authentic patient interaction photography (children, teens, adults).
- Modern clinical equipment: iTero Element 5D intraoral scanners, clear aligner smartcases, digital treatment simulations.
- Natural-light photography with neutral photographic grading (`saturate(0.95)`).
